householdplanner-bot

householdplanner-bot is the research agent of Household Planner, a private planner one household runs for itself. It reads public fund fact sheets, one page at a time, to sort the household's own funds into stocks, bonds and the rest.

  • It obeys robots.txt. To keep it out, add User-agent: householdplanner-bot and Disallow: / to your robots.txt.
  • It waits between requests to the same site and reads only a few pages from a site each day.
  • It never logs in, never reads pages about private individuals, and never works around a site that refuses it.
  • It always identifies itself with this name and this page.

To reach the person who runs it: open an issue at github.com/theavikumar/householdplanner-bot/issues.
